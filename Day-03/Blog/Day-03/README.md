## 💡 What I Learned

Data cleaning is one of the most important stages in the data analytics lifecycle.

Before performing any analysis, it's essential to ensure that the dataset is accurate, consistent, and free from errors. Even small issues like duplicate records, blank cells, or inconsistent formatting can lead to incorrect insights.

Excel provides several powerful built-in tools that make cleaning data faster and more efficient.
