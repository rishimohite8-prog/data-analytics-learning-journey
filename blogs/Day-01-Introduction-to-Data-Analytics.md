# 📖 What is Data?

Data is a collection of raw facts, figures, or observations that can be processed to generate meaningful information. It can come from various sources, including business transactions, websites, mobile applications, sensors, surveys, and social media platforms.

Examples of data include:

* Customer information
* Sales records
* Website traffic
* Employee details
* Financial transactions

On its own, raw data has limited value. When it is cleaned, organized, and analyzed, it becomes useful information that supports better decision-making.

---

# 📊 What is Data Analytics?

Data Analytics is the process of collecting, cleaning, transforming, analyzing, and visualizing data to uncover meaningful insights and support informed decision-making.

Organizations rely on data analytics to answer questions such as:

* Which products generate the highest sales?
* Why are sales decreasing in a specific region?
* Which customers are most likely to purchase again?
* How can business operations become more efficient?

Instead of making decisions based on assumptions, organizations use data-driven insights to improve outcomes.

---

# 📈 Types of Data Analytics

### 1. Descriptive Analytics

Answers the question:
**"What happened?"**

Example:
A company analyzes monthly sales reports to understand total revenue generated during the month.

---

### 2. Diagnostic Analytics

Answers the question:
**"Why did it happen?"**

Example:
Sales decreased because a popular product was out of stock.

---

### 3. Predictive Analytics

Answers the question:
**"What is likely to happen?"**

Example:
Forecasting next month's sales using historical data.

---

### 4. Prescriptive Analytics

Answers the question:
**"What should we do?"**

Example:
Recommending promotional offers to increase sales in underperforming regions.

---

# 🔄 Data Analytics Workflow

A typical analytics process follows these steps:

1. Collect data from different sources.
2. Clean and prepare the data.
3. Analyze the data using statistical or analytical techniques.
4. Visualize insights through charts and dashboards.
5. Make informed business decisions.

This structured approach helps transform raw data into actionable insights.

---

# 👨‍💼 Role of a Data Analyst

A Data Analyst bridges the gap between raw data and business decisions. Their responsibilities include:

* Collecting and organizing data.
* Cleaning inaccurate or incomplete data.
* Identifying trends and patterns.
* Building reports and dashboards.
* Presenting insights to stakeholders.
* Supporting data-driven decision-making.

# 🌍 Real-World Applications of Data Analytics

Data Analytics is used across many industries, including:

* Retail – Understanding customer purchasing behavior.
