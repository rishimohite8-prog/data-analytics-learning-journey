# Day 37 — Tableau: Advanced HR Analytics

## Overview

Today I extended my HR analytics work in Tableau by focusing on workforce behavior, employee retention, and factors that may be associated with attrition.

Instead of looking at attrition as a single percentage, I analyzed it across multiple employee dimensions.

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Monthly Attrition Trends

I analyzed employee attrition over time to identify changes in workforce turnover and possible periods of increased attrition.

### 2. Department and Job-Role Analysis

Attrition was compared across departments and job roles using heatmap-style analysis to make high-attrition areas easier to identify.

### 3. Salary vs. Tenure

I explored the relationship between employee salary and tenure to understand compensation patterns across different experience levels.

### 4. Performance and Overtime

Performance ratings and overtime information were analyzed alongside attrition to explore possible relationships between work conditions, performance, and employee retention.

### 5. Promotion History

Promotion history was included to investigate how career progression may relate to employee retention.

### 6. Recruitment Source

Different recruitment sources were compared to understand whether employees entering through different channels showed different retention patterns.

### 7. Employee Satisfaction

Employee satisfaction was analyzed as another important dimension of workforce behavior.

## Tableau Techniques Used

* Calculated fields
* Heatmaps
* Filters
* Interactive dashboards
* KPI analysis
* Time-series analysis
* Comparative visualizations
* Dimension-based segmentation

## Key Learning

A single attrition percentage provides limited information.

Attrition becomes much more useful when broken down by:

* Tenure
* Department
* Job role
* Salary
* Performance
* Overtime
* Promotions
* Satisfaction
* Recruitment source

This type of multidimensional analysis can reveal patterns that may remain hidden in a simple summary.

## Conclusion

This exercise improved my understanding of advanced Tableau analysis and showed how interactive dashboards can turn workforce data into a more detailed data story.
