# Day 36 — Tableau: Deep HR Data Analysis

## Overview

Today I worked on a deep HR analytics dashboard using Tableau. The objective was to transform employee-level data into meaningful workforce insights that could support HR decision-making.

The analysis focused on employee distribution, attrition, compensation, tenure, satisfaction, gender, and employment status.

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Employee Distribution by Department

I analyzed the number of employees across different departments to understand workforce composition and identify departments with higher or lower employee concentration.

### 2. Attrition Analysis

Attrition was analyzed across:

* Department
* Age group
* Employment categories

This helped identify areas where employee turnover may require further investigation.

### 3. Salary Distribution by Job Role

Salary patterns were compared across different job roles to understand compensation differences and identify potential salary distribution patterns.

### 4. Employee Tenure and Job Satisfaction

I compared employee tenure with job satisfaction to explore whether employee experience and satisfaction showed noticeable patterns.

### 5. Gender and Employment Status

The dashboard also examined gender distribution and employment status to provide a broader view of workforce composition.

## Tableau Features Used

* Calculated fields
* Filters
* Interactive dashboards
* KPI cards
* Charts and comparisons
* Data aggregation
* Dashboard-level analysis

## Key Learning

A dashboard should not simply contain multiple charts. Each visualization should answer a meaningful business question.

The major learning from this exercise was the importance of combining appropriate KPIs, filters, and visualizations to help users identify workforce patterns quickly.

## Business Applications

This type of HR dashboard can support analysis related to:

* Employee retention
* Workforce planning
* Hiring
* Compensation
* Employee experience
* Department-level workforce management

## Conclusion

This project helped me understand how Tableau can transform raw employee records into an interactive analytical view that supports data-driven HR discussions.
