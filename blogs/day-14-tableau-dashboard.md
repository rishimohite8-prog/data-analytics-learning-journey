# Day 14 — Building an Interactive Tableau Dashboard

## What I Learned Today

Today I moved beyond simply analyzing data and focused on presenting insights through an interactive **Tableau dashboard**.

The goal was not just to create charts, but to understand how different visualizations can work together to tell a meaningful data story.

## Practical Work

Today I worked on:

* Connecting a dataset to Tableau
* Understanding dimensions and measures
* Creating charts and visualizations
* Applying filters for interactive analysis
* Comparing important business metrics
* Arranging multiple visualizations into a dashboard
* Improving dashboard readability and layout
* Using visualizations to identify patterns and trends

## Key Learning

One important lesson from today's work was:

> A dashboard is not just a collection of charts. It should help someone understand the data and make a decision quickly.

I focused on choosing visualizations based on the question being answered rather than creating charts simply because they look attractive.

## What I Improved

Before today, I mainly focused on preparing and analyzing data.

Today I started thinking about the final stage:

**Data → Analysis → Visualization → Insight → Decision**

This helped me understand the role of BI tools in real-world data analytics.

## Takeaway

Today was another step toward becoming a better data analyst.

The technical skill is important, but learning how to communicate insights clearly through dashboards is equally important.

**Day 14 complete.**
